📖 IHCC Elo System

K-Factor: 32

Expected score: E = 1 ÷ (1 + 10(Opponent Rating − Your Rating) ÷ 400)

New rating: Old Rating + 32 × (Actual Result − Expected Result)

Win = 1; Loss = 0; No Result = no Elo change.


Ranking Points and Elo Rating are separate values.
