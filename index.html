📖 IHCC Elo Ranking Rules

Elo K-Factor: 32
Elo ratings are updated according to match results. A win increases the winner's rating and decreases the loser's rating, based on their pre-match ratings.

1. Expected Score

Expected score estimates the probability of a team winning based on the difference between the teams' current Elo ratings.

2. Elo Formula

Expected Score = 1 / (1 + 10((Opponent Elo − Team Elo) / 400))

New Elo = Old Elo + K × (Actual Score − Expected Score)

3. Match Outcomes

  • Win: Actual score = 1
  • Loss: Actual score = 0
  • No Result: Elo rating remains unchanged

4. IHCC Ranking Points

Win: +10 points
Reach the semifinal: +30 points
Reach the final: +40 points
Win the tournament: +50 points

Tournament-stage bonuses should be entered separately when recording the relevant achievements.

5. Match and Tournament Records

Bilateral, tri-national and quadrangular series can affect match counts, ratings and ranking points. They should not be displayed as World Cup tournaments in the tournament archive. World Cup tournaments are recorded in the tournament archive.
